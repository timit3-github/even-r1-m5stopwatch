Release v0.3.2: Touch Toggle and Scroll Repeat

Release date: 2026-10-15
Project version: 0.3.2
App compatibility string: 2.9.9.9999 (not stock R1 firmware)
Source-only distribution; ESP-IDF 5.5.1.
Persistent touch toggle with one/two vibration pulses.
G2 short-then-hold down repeat and fixed-direction touch swipe repeat.
Haptic initialization retries at boot and on the next toggle after failure.
Same-code development build: touch toggle, NVS retention, repeats and vibration after power cycling confirmed.
Final v0.3.2 host tests and ESP32-S3 build passed.
Additional-feature endurance and repeated power-cycle stability remain unverified.
See docs/releases/v0.3.2.md, docs/STOPWATCH.md and docs/VALIDATION.md.
